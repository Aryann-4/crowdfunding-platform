Good to know

Questions, answered

The details behind the demo and the contract.

Is the demo using real funds?

No. This demo runs entirely in your browser with simulated ETH and USDC balances. The underlying Solidity contracts and 65-test suite live in this repository.

What happens if a campaign misses its goal?

Pledges stay in the contract but become refundable. Each backer pulls their own refund after the deadline — no creator action required.

Can I withdraw before the deadline?

Yes. While a campaign is live, backers can unpledge part or all of their contribution. After the deadline, settlement rules apply.

Which tokens are supported?

Native ETH and any standard ERC-20 token. Campaigns are denominated in a single asset chosen at launch.
